Pressure & calibration notes +

The barometer measures the air pressure at the station’s elevation. This page also offers a standard-atmosphere sea-level estimate, using a configured height of — m. That estimate is not a calibrated meteorological sea-level observation.

Three-hour pressure changes help describe a developing pattern. A falling barometer can accompany unsettled weather; a rising one can accompany improving conditions. These indications are not a local forecast.

Pressure calibration stays separate from elevation adjustment. Local sensor readings remain available in the graph selector. How pressure measurements differ ↗
